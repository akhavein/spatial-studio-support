What to expect from room-scanning LiDAR

Plan around ±5 cm (about 2 in) for scanned dimensions.

This is a typical working tolerance used by Spatial Studio to set expectations, not a guarantee for every wall or room. Some scans can differ by more, especially in difficult conditions.

Spatial Studio rounds scanned dimensions to avoid false precision: 1 cm in metric or ½ inch in imperial. The editor lets you replace an important scanned value with a tape-measured value and identifies that value as user-set.

No measurement from Spatial Studio should be treated as a certified survey, appraisal, as-built drawing, or code-compliance measurement.

Know where a number came from

Value type How it is produced How to use it
Scanned dimension Estimated from the on-device RoomPlan capture; displayed to 1 cm or ½ in. Good for planning and documentation where approximate dimensions are acceptable. Verify critical values.
User-set dimension Typed by you from a tape or another source and marked separately in the editor. Used as the entered value in the plan and locked against geometry changes until you choose to unlock it.
Calculated area Computed from the interior floor polygon in the edited plan. An interior floor-area estimate only; not an appraisal or listing-area standard.

Conditions that affect a scan

LiDAR is only one input. RoomPlan also relies on camera imagery, device tracking, and a clear view of room boundaries. Accuracy and completeness can be affected by:

  • mirrors, glass, glossy surfaces, dark corners, or poor lighting;
  • furniture or clutter that hides wall edges and corners;
  • fast movement, interrupted tracking, or incomplete wall coverage;
  • very large, curved, angled, or open spaces;
  • doors and windows that are partly obscured;
  • separate room scans that must be manually aligned.

For better results, move slowly, keep the device roughly 2–3 metres from each wall, sweep from top to bottom, and capture every corner. Review the plan before exporting.

How area is calculated

Spatial Studio reports interior floor area from the plan’s net room polygon. It is not ANSI Z765 gross living area (GLA), exterior area, appraisal square footage, or a representation that a space meets any legal definition of habitable area.

Area changes when the underlying geometry changes, so scan error, manual alignment, and edits can all affect the total. Do not copy this figure into a listing, valuation, permit, insurance filing, purchase decision, or contract without applying the required standard and independent verification.

When independent verification matters

Always verify critical dimensions with a tape, laser measure, or qualified professional before:

  • ordering cabinetry, glass, countertops, flooring, or fabricated components;
  • making structural, electrical, plumbing, accessibility, or code decisions;
  • using a measurement in an appraisal, property listing, insurance claim, permit, or legal filing;
  • making a purchase or construction decision where an error would be costly or unsafe.

Spatial Studio is a documentation and planning tool. It does not provide surveying, architecture, engineering, appraisal, or other professional services.
