A direct workflow

From room to floor plan in three steps.

Capture with Apple’s RoomPlan, refine the result in a purpose-built editor, and share a finished plan without sending your room to a service.

01

Scan

Walk the room with a supported LiDAR-equipped iPhone or iPad. Camera and LiDAR data are processed locally.

02

Fix

Adjust walls, doors, windows, openings, labels, and notes. Type critical measurements from a tape and keep them locked.

03

Export

Create dimensioned plans, image files, CAD-ready DXF, or 3D OBJ and USDZ files, then share with the system share sheet.

Nothing hidden

Your rooms are not our data.

Spatial Studio has no developer server, account system, advertising, or analytics SDK. The app’s App Store privacy response is Data Not Collected.

Read the full privacy policy →

  • Local projects Your scans, edits, exports, and diagnostics remain in local files until you choose to share them.
  • Apple handles purchases StoreKit, payment, purchase history, and refund requests are handled by Apple.
  • External links are your choice Support and refund websites open only when you select a link or button.

Measure honestly.

Phone-based LiDAR measurements are estimates. Spatial Studio makes scanned and typed dimensions distinct, but critical measurements still need verification with a tape or by a qualified professional.
