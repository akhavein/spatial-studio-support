Plain-language policy

Privacy means we do not get your data.

Spatial Studio processes room scans and project files on your device. The app has no developer server, no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Effective and last updated: July 31, 2026

On this page Summary Data on your device Purchases External links This website Your control Changes and contact

The short version

App Store privacy response: Data Not Collected.

The Spatial Studio app does not transmit scans, project content, diagnostics, identifiers, usage activity, or purchase information to us. It makes no app-initiated network requests.

Apple’s operating system may communicate with Apple for App Store and StoreKit functions. If you deliberately open an external support or refund link, your browser connects to that external service. Those actions are separate from Spatial Studio’s local scan and editing workflow.

Data that stays on your device

Spatial Studio uses the camera and LiDAR sensor to create a model of a room. It stores the resulting scan data, edits, notes, project metadata, previews, and generated exports in files on your device. The app does not upload those files to us or to a Spatial Studio cloud service; no such service exists.

Local diagnostics

The app can keep a limited local log of recent app events to help diagnose a problem. It is not sent automatically. You may copy it and decide whether to include it in a support request. You can also clear it in the app.

Device settings

The app uses local device storage for preferences and file-management functions, such as unit choices, onboarding state, file timestamps, and available disk space. This information is used on the device and is not collected by us.

Purchases are handled by Apple

The optional Pro unlock uses Apple’s StoreKit system. Apple processes your Apple ID, payment method, purchase history, Family Sharing status, and refund request under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. Spatial Studio receives the verified entitlement status needed to unlock Pro on your device. It does not send that status to a developer server.

Refund decisions and payment support are provided by Apple. See the refund instructions for the user-initiated options.

External links open only when you choose

Spatial Studio may offer links to this support site, a GitHub support issue, or Apple’s refund service. The app does not silently load these websites. A connection to an external service begins only after you select its link or button, and that service’s privacy practices then apply.

This support website

This is a static website. It contains no analytics script, advertising, tracking pixel, contact form, third-party font, or cookie set by Spatial Studio.

The site is designed for GitHub Pages hosting. As the hosting provider, GitHub may receive standard web request information such as an IP address, browser details, and access time under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive a separate analytics feed from this site.

Your files, retention, and deletion

You control the project files on your device. Spatial Studio project packages are visible through the Files app, and exports go only where you choose through the system share sheet.

  • Delete a project in your file storage when you no longer need it.
  • Clear the local diagnostics log from Spatial Studio settings.
  • Keep device backups through iCloud device backup or a computer if you want recovery protection.
  • Deleting the app deletes project data still stored inside its app container. Copies you previously saved or shared elsewhere remain where you placed them.

Because we do not receive your app data, there is no Spatial Studio account or server record for us to access, export, or erase.

Policy changes and contact

If Spatial Studio’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released, and the date at the top will be revised. A change that introduces collection would also require an updated App Store privacy response.

For a privacy question, open a support issue on GitHub. GitHub issues are public: do not include an address, a room scan, a project file, or other sensitive information.
